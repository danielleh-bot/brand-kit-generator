How this was produced

Brand tokens: the colors, typography, logo, buttons, and layout above are Business Insider's documented visual identity, packaged as a reusable kit.

Visual brand tokens (colors, fonts, logo, buttons, photo style, header) are reconciled from a live Puppeteer crawl of businessinsider.com — primary accent #002AFF (blue, not the legacy orange), Garnett headline/UI type with a Tiempos editorial serif, white masthead, square 0px corners. The article context (headline, deck, byline, hero, body, related cards) is the requested Cursor/Truell story; BI's chrome tokens are publisher-level and apply across articles. A handful of obvious mis-extractions were corrected and noted inline: article_title_hero and buttons font sizes, button horizontal padding, and the graphics badge list (page UI chrome was dropped, keeping the real coloured CTAs and deriving LIVE/BREAKING/VIDEO from content_labels).
