Lionel Messi's post-World Cup final tears illustrate the belated but beautiful bond Argentina's icon discovered with his homeland

Krishan Davis · 21 Jul 2026 03:00-04:00

Lionel Messi didn't always have a great relationship with the Argentina national team, but the tears that followed Sunday's defeat in the World Cup final highlighted the beautiful bond he has found with the Albiceleste in recent years.

There is a sense that we are awaiting the inevitable after Argentina were downed by Spain in the 2026 World Cup final. The tears rolling down Lionel Messi's face after the full-time whistle in New Jersey suggested he may well be about to call time on his glittering international career, having powered his country through the tournament in North America at the age of 39.

Beginning of the end

For once, Messi was peripheral, with his team-mates adopting an uber-physical approach in a failed attempt to rattle La Roja, rather than trying to play to his strengths. Given the defeat, and the nature of it, this would undoubtedly be a sad end to an incredibly special symbiosis between player and country.

Defeat in Sunday's final hurt him a lot, as Messi described the pain caused by the result as "immense, and it will take time for this wound to heal", but whatever he decides to do next, he can take solace in the fact his legacy has been secure for some time.

It's always worth remembering that things could have panned out very differently for Messi, and indeed the rest of the football world; in an alternate timeline, his international career ended a decade ago.
