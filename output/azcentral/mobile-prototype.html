Raytheon wins contract to expand missile production in Tucson

RTX's Raytheon division will significantly ramp up production capacity for five key munitions, adding hundreds of jobs to its Southern Arizona operations.

RW
Ryan Winkleman
Arizona Republic | June 4, 2025

Raytheon's Tucson campus will expand production of Tomahawk and AMRAAM missiles under new multiyear agreements with the Department of Defense. Photo: RTX Corp.

Raytheon's manufacturing output in Tucson is set to ramp up significantly over the next several years after the RTX division secured a set of multiyear agreements with the U.S. Department of Defense to expand production capacity and deliveries of five key munitions.

Under the agreements, Raytheon will increase annual output of Land Attack and Maritime Strike variants of Tomahawk missiles, with production rising to more than 1,000 units annually. AMRAAM missiles, the most widely deployed air-to-air missiles in the world, will see production increase to at least 1,900 units a year.

The expansion is expected to bring hundreds of new jobs to Southern Arizona, with work also connected to Raytheon facilities in Alabama and Massachusetts. The deals underscore Tucson's growing importance as a critical node in the nation's defense industrial base.

"This investment reflects our commitment to ensuring America's warfighters have the advanced munitions they need, when they need them," said a Raytheon spokesperson. The Tucson facility has been a cornerstone of the company's missile production capabilities for decades.
