Projecting the USA's 2026 World Cup Roster Following Losses To Portugal, Belgium

DM
Doug McIntyre
April 3, 2026

With the 2026 FIFA World Cup set to kick off on home soil in just two months, the United States men's national team is running out of time to sort out its roster. The recent friendly losses to Portugal and Belgium have only intensified the debate over which 26 players will be named to the final squad.

Head coach Gregg Berhalter faces several difficult decisions, particularly in the midfield and at center back. While Christian Pulisic, Weston McKennie, and Tim Weah appear to be locks, the battle for the remaining spots has never been more fierce.
