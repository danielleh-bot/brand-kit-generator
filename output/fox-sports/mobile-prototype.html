World Cup Roundup: Stars Shine Tuesday, Headlined by Lionel Messi Hat Trick

FS
FOX Sports Staff
June 17, 2026

Tuesday at the 2026 FIFA World Cup was one for the ages. Lionel Messi reminded the world why he's the greatest to ever do it, netting a hat trick as Argentina cruised past their Group C opponents. The 39-year-old magician rolled back the years with three goals of increasing brilliance.

But Messi wasn't the only star to shine. Kylian Mbappé scored twice for France, Jude Bellingham orchestrated England's midfield masterclass, and hosts USA got a late equalizer from Christian Pulisic to set up a thrilling group stage finale.
